Our Story

Our Story

Two wanderers. One incredible journey.

Chapter 1

Where It All Began

We were lucky enough to meet in March 2021 through a mutual friend. Harry had known this friend from school and kept in touch, Hannah had met this friend whilst she was in sixth form. Luckily enough she suggested setting us up for a little date. One date later and we have been inseparable ever since. We thank our lucky stars and our friend every day for bringing us together.

Chapter 2

When We Booked Our First Trip

We booked our very first trip to Iceland back in July 2021 for January 2022. That following month we booked 3 more trips - which then gave us the inspiration of travel. Especially after covid as our trips planned for the end of 2021 sadly got cancelled which just made our travel bug worse.

Planning
Chapter 3

Taking the Leap

We finally make it to our first new country together in January 2022 in Iceland and immediately fell in love with the beautiful landscapes

So we made a plan. Start up a travel blog, write down our journeys to share to the world and inspire more people to travel

It took a long time to set up, but we finally launched our website and began exploring the world at a whole new level.

49 Countries Later

Today

The Journey Continues

Almost four years later, we're still going. We've learned that travel isn't about ticking countries off a list—it's about the people you meet, the perspectives you gain, and the person you become.

We've slept in very questionable hotels, luxury cruises, and everything in between. We've eaten street food that changed our lives and fine dining that didn't. We've gotten lost, found our way, and gotten lost again on purpose.

This blog is our way of sharing these moments with you. The good, the challenging, the hilarious, and the profound. Because the world is too beautiful not to explore, and every journey is better when shared.
