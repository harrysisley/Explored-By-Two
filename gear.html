Our Packing Philosophy

🎒

Pack Light

If you can't carry it for 30 minutes, you're bringing too much. We each travel with just a 40L backpack.

👕

Quality Over Quantity

Buy fewer, better items. One great merino shirt beats five cheap cotton ones.

🔄

Multi-Purpose Items

Every item should serve multiple purposes. Our sarong is a towel, blanket, and beach cover-up.
